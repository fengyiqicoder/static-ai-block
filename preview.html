This preview is a small page about sourdough. A starter is flour and water left to ferment; it leavens bread with wild yeast and bacteria instead of commercial yeast. Feed it daily at room temperature or weekly in the fridge. A loaf needs about 20% starter, a long cold proof of 12 to 18 hours, and a very hot oven, 250 °C, with steam for the first 20 minutes.
